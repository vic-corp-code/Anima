Adoption

Four steps to bringing them home

Adopting a shelter animal is a real decision, and we've made the path through it as calm and clear as we can. Here's exactly what happens, from the first browse to the first night home — no surprises, no pressure.

Most adoptions take 1–3 weeks · Fee waived for our longest-staying animals

The process

From first look to first night

Four small steps. We're with you at each one, and you can step back at any point — no hard feelings.

  1. 01

    Browse

    Look through the animals available online or in person and shortlist a few that catch your eye. Save your favourites and we'll have them ready when you visit.

    Typical duration~ an afternoon

  2. 02

    Meet

    Visit the shelter and spend 30–45 minutes with the animal in our meet room. Everyone who'll live with them should come along, children included.

    Typical durationsame day

  3. 03

    Home check

    A short conversation, in person or over video, about your home, your daily routine, and any other pets. We help you prep — we're not here to grade you.

    Typical durationwithin a week

  4. 04

    Take them home

    Sign the adoption agreement, settle the fee, and head home together. We follow up at two weeks, two months, and six months to see how everyone's settling in.

    Typical durationthe best part

Included with every adoption

What comes home with them

Every animal leaves the shelter ready for a healthy start — the medical work is already done before you meet them.

  • Spay or neuter surgery
  • Initial vaccinations
  • Microchip registration
  • Health check & flea/worm treatment
  • Starter bag of food
  • ID tag & starter collar

Fees

A contribution, not a price

The adoption fee covers a fraction of the care each animal has already received. The amounts below are examples for this prototype.

Animal type Example fee Covers
Dogs $185* Neuter, vaccinations, microchip, and health check.
Cats $95* Neuter, vaccinations, microchip, and health check.
Small animals (rabbits, guinea pigs) $35* Health check and starter care.

* Example amounts shown for this prototype — not official fees. Our longest-staying animals (Duke, Whiskers, and Buddy) have their fee waived entirely; if one of them turns out to be your match, there's nothing to pay.

Before you visit

Setting up for a smooth adoption

A little prep goes a long way. Here's what we'll talk through, and a few things worth knowing in advance.

We'll ask about

  • Your home setup — house or flat, garden, stairs, and any limits in your lease.
  • Your daily routine and roughly how long the animal would be alone.
  • Existing pets, and how they've met other animals before.
  • Everyone in the household, including visiting children and regular guests.

Good to know

  • Returns are always allowed, no questions asked, at any point in the animal's life.
  • Foster-to-adopt is possible for some animals — a trial fortnight before you commit.
  • We support you for the animal's whole life: behaviour advice, vet contacts, and a friendly ear.

Questions

People often ask

And if yours isn't here, just ask us — by email, phone, or in person at the shelter.

Can I adopt if I rent?
Yes — we don't require homeownership. We may ask about your lease or your landlord so we can help you confirm any pet restrictions before you fall in love with an animal who might not be allowed.
How long does approval take?
Most applications are decided within two to four days. The home check is usually the only waiting step, and we'll arrange it at a time that suits you — in person or over video.
What if it doesn't work out?
You can bring the animal back at any point, no questions asked. We'd far rather help you through a hard patch, or welcome them back, than have either of you struggle in silence.
Do you ship animals?
No. Adoptions happen in person at the shelter so you can meet first and travel home together the same day. If you live a long way off, talk to us — we can sometimes arrange to meet partway.
Can I adopt as a gift?
Not as a surprise. The person who'll care for the animal needs to meet them and be part of the decision — an animal is a commitment of many years, not a present. We're happy to arrange a surprise "meet voucher" instead.
What about young children?
Bring them along. We want every person — and every existing pet — who'll share the home to be part of the meet, so we can match you with an animal whose temperament fits your family.

Ready to meet them?

Shortlist a few, then come and spend an afternoon with them in our meet room. We'll take it from there — together.
