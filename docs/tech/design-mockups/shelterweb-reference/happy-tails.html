Adopted · January 2026
Nine years old, 241 days with us — and now the only dog in a very quiet house in Machesney Park.

— Rocky's family

Rocky came to us in late spring, surrendered when his owner could no longer keep him. Nine years old, slow on the stairs, particular about his dinner. For 241 days he was our longest resident — the one every volunteer walked first and last, the one whose kennel card we kept rewriting because "senior" never quite captured him.

Then a family from Machesney Park came in looking for a calm older dog. They took him home on a trial weekend and never brought him back. He has a specific couch cushion now. He has a route.
