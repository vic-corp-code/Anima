Other ways in

Not ready to adopt? There is still plenty to do

01

Volunteer a morning

Dog walking, laundry, kitten socialising, weekend reception. Training is one Saturday session and there is no minimum commitment beyond turning up when you say you will.

Ask about shifts
02

Foster for a few weeks

Kittens under eight weeks, dogs recovering from surgery, animals who simply do badly in kennels. We supply food, crates, medication and a phone number you can call at 2am.

Join the foster list
03

Give, or send supplies

Right now we need blankets, unopened kitten milk replacer and gravel cat litter. Cash goes straight into the veterinary fund — last year that covered 74 operations.
